## Vision & Problem Statement

**Problem:** Active people who care about their fitness are not enjoying their workouts. Training plans are boring, repetitive, and poorly matched — existing tools are either too simple (step counters) or too complex (Hevy, TrainingPeaks). The gap lies in between.

**Pain points:**

- Friction in the workflow — too many clicks, too many options, too little adaptation to the individual
- Decision paralysis — not knowing which plan to pick, so nothing gets started at all
- Boredom from repetition — a plan works technically, but stops being motivating after a few weeks

**Insight:** AI opens up personalization possibilities that until recently required a live coach. This shifts the cost of delivering a personalized plan from "expensive expert" to "tailored algorithm."

## User & Persona

**Primary Persona:** An active person who consciously takes care of their fitness.

- Exercises regularly or wants to, but is not a trainer or professional athlete
- Knows the basics, wants to progress — does not want to read manuals or configure apps
- Experiences friction when it comes time to plan or start a workout: not knowing where to begin, or feeling bored by what they already know
- Scale: individual users (not organizations)

## Access Control

- **Auth model:** Email + password / OAuth (user account, data stored server-side)
- **Role model:** Flat — all logged-in users have identical permissions; each user sees only their own data
- **No admin panel in MVP** — exercise library imported by the developer outside the app flow (in line with the idea notes)

## Success Criteria

### Primary

The user can complete the full end-to-end flow:

1. Register / log in
2. Complete the onboarding survey (goal, experience level, available equipment, preferred style)
3. Receive a personalized training plan generated by AI based on the survey
4. Launch a workout and log sets, reps, and weights in real time
5. Finish the workout and see it on a chronological history list

### Secondary

Indicate which exercise improved compared to the previous workout (simple inline information on the history / workout screen).

### Guardrails

- Saved workout data (sets, reps, weights) must be persistent — losing history disqualifies the product
- User data is isolated — each user sees only their own workouts and results

## User Stories

### US-01: First Workout After the Survey

**Given** the user has completed the onboarding survey
**When** they open the "My Plan" section
**Then** they see a weekly training plan generated by AI, tailored to their goal, experience level, and available equipment

### US-02: Logging Sets During a Workout

**Given** the user has launched a workout session
**When** they finish a set of an exercise
**Then** they can enter the number of reps and weight, and the data is saved immediately

### US-03: Browsing History

**Given** the user has completed at least one workout
**When** they open the "History" section
**Then** they see a chronological list of workouts with dates and recorded results

## Business Logic

**Domain rule:** The system selects the exercise set, session order, and difficulty level based on the user's training goal, experience level, available equipment, and preferred style.

**Rule inputs (from the user's perspective):**

- Training goal (e.g., strength, muscle mass, cardio, fat loss)
- Experience level (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced)
- Available equipment (e.g., full gym, home dumbbells, no equipment)
- Preferred style (e.g., full-body workout, split, circuit)
- Sessions per week (selected in the survey)

**Rule output:** A weekly plan with N sessions — each session is an ordered list of exercises from the library, matched to the user's profile.

**V1 vs V2 boundary:**

- V1: AI relies solely on the static profile from the survey
- V2 (out of MVP scope): AI adapts the plan based on workout history — what the user actually did, with what weight, and how often

## Non-Functional Requirements

- Response time for workout actions (logging a set, loading history): perceptibly instant, p95 < 1s
- Continuous visible feedback during any operation lasting > 2s (especially AI plan generation)
- Browser support: Chrome and Safari (latest versions)

## Open Questions

- **OQ-001:** One-time survey vs. ability to update the profile — if the user changes their goal or equipment, the current plan becomes outdated. A minimal edit option (e.g., "reset profile and retake the survey") may be needed sooner than V2. To be resolved during implementation.

## Non-Goals

- Adaptive AI (history analysis, dynamic weight progression suggestions) — this is V2. V1 AI operates solely on the static profile from the survey.
- Full calendar view — a visual grid of days and months. A simple history list is sufficient for MVP.
- Social module — sharing workouts, competing with friends. The product is private / solo.
- Monetization — ads, payments, subscriptions. The app is intended to be free / private.
